# Slingshot Advisory — Service Expansion Roadmap (INTERNAL)

**Internal planning document — not published, not linked publicly, not advertised.**
Phase 3 offerings must **not** be presented anywhere on the site as currently available.

The public site positions Slingshot Advisory as an operator-led **business, technology,
and real estate consulting** company. The goal right now is to **validate customer demand**
before investing in additional licensing, brokerage affiliations, or operational
infrastructure. Start with what we can competently and legally deliver; expand only when
the opportunity justifies the overhead.

> A salesperson license that is inactive or not affiliated with an appropriate broker does
> **not** authorize brokerage activity. Marketing and contracting must reflect the business's
> **actual** authorization, not planned future capabilities.

---

## Phase 1 — Current advisory (live today)

What we can competently and legally deliver now. These are what the site markets.

- Digital development & automation ($750 custom websites, $35/mo optional care, AI/automation)
- Business strategy & branding
- Financial modeling / real estate underwriting (NOI, cap rate, DSCR, cash flow, refi scenarios)
- Commercial lease & occupancy cost analysis (CAM reconciliation reviews, renewal modeling)
- Commercial property performance & repositioning — **analysis and strategy only**
  (performance assessment, repositioning strategy, financial analysis, technology & operations)

Boundary: no brokerage, tenant representation, leasing, lease negotiation, tenant/space
procurement for compensation, rent collection for others, or third-party property management.

## Phase 3 — Licensed service expansion (NOT offered; do not advertise)

## Regulatory notes (flag for a Minnesota real estate attorney)

Minnesota real estate brokerage definitions can extend beyond selling property. Some leasing,
negotiation, tenant procurement, property management, and transaction-related advisory
activities may require appropriate licensing. Calling an activity "consulting," "strategy," or
"asset management" does **not** automatically exempt it from licensing.

Items to review with counsel before offering:
- Any performance- or revenue-linked fee tied to a property's leasing/occupancy/revenue.
- The boundary between "repositioning strategy / marketing concepts" and activities that
  constitute procuring tenants or negotiating leases for compensation.
- Whether ongoing "property performance" retainers could be construed as property management.
- Public-facing claims and intake-form options must reflect actual authorization.

Do not invent regulatory approvals, licenses, or brokerage affiliations.
